# iDma Product Overview

[Back to resource center](../README.md) · [Images and downloads](../ASSET_GUIDE.md)

September 8, 2026

iDma's product range combines paper-based handwriting notebooks with gateways and collaboration software for meetings, teaching, and remote participation.

This overview edits the supplied descriptions into consistent English. Features and accessories below are reported in the [September 8 specification workbook](IDMA_BH_Product_Spec_Sheet%2020260908.xlsx), worksheet `产品清单与图片`, rows 2–7. Confirm the intended software configuration and final package contents when preparing a listing or order.

## Handwriting notebooks

| Model | Format and material | Intended workflow | Writing-area behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PP-100** | Vertical, leather | Frequent meetings, optical character recognition (OCR), and notes linked to recordings | Public and private writing zones. The source recommends paper with a divider: the upper half is shared with participants and the lower half is visible to the writer only. |
| **PP-110** | Horizontal A4, leather | Desktop annotation, training, and hybrid-office presentations | The entire writing area is shared with participants. |
| **PP-120** | Vertical, polymer; described as vertical A4 in the specification text | Math, science, and remote tutoring | Public and private writing zones, with the same divided-paper recommendation as PP-100. |

**Accessories listed for each notebook:** digital pen, replacement nibs, and USB charging cable. Exact accessory quantities are not specified.

Writing-zone descriptions refer to the collaboration behavior stated in the source. Device dimensions, supported software versions, recording requirements, and detailed privacy controls are not specified in this workbook.

Use the base SKU in item setup. Lite, Pro, and Max are the corresponding variant names shown in the specification workbook.
